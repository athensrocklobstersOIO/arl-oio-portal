📖 Rules & Procedures

Reference information, operating procedures, and expectations for Athens Rock Lobsters Off-Ice Officials.

OIO Expectations

  • Arrive on time and be prepared for your assigned position.
  • Follow instructions provided by the OIO supervisor.
  • Maintain accurate and timely game information.
  • Communicate professionally with other game personnel.
  • Maintain confidentiality regarding official game information.
  • Report problems or unusual situations to the supervisor.

Professional Conduct

Off-Ice Officials are part of the official game operations team. Officials should remain professional, neutral, attentive, and respectful throughout the event.

Remember

OIOs should avoid interfering with on-ice officiating, team operations, or other game personnel unless their assigned responsibilities require communication.

Game Information

Official Game Record

All official game information should be recorded accurately and reviewed before the game is finalized.

Statistics

Statistics should be recorded consistently and checked for accuracy throughout the game.

Penalties

Penalty information should be recorded and communicated accurately according to the applicable game procedures.

Communication

When information is unclear, confirm it with the appropriate game official or OIO supervisor rather than making assumptions.
