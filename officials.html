Off-Ice Official Roles

📊 Data & Statistics

Records official game statistics, including goals, assists, shots, penalties, and other required events.

⏱️ Scorekeeper

Maintains the official scoresheet and ensures game information is recorded accurately.

🕐 Timekeeper

Operates the game clock and penalty clocks and monitors official game time.

📺 Penalty Box

Manages the penalty box, tracks player penalties, and communicates with the timekeeper and officials.

🥅 Goal Judge

Supports goal determination and game operations as assigned.

💻 Game Operations

Supports game-management systems, electronic scoring, statistics, and other required technology.

📋 Support Roles

Additional OIO responsibilities may be assigned depending on the game, staffing, or league requirements.
