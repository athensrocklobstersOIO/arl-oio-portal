🏒 Game Day

Everything you need to know before, during, and after an Athens Rock Lobsters game.

Before You Arrive

  • Review your game assignment.
  • Confirm your reporting time.
  • Review any special game-day instructions.
  • Make sure you have your required equipment.
  • Arrive early enough to be ready for your assignment.

Arrival & Check-In

Upon arrival at the arena, check in with the Off-Ice Officials supervisor and confirm your assigned position.

Important:

Your supervisor may provide additional instructions depending on the game, opponent, or league requirements.

Position Responsibilities

Scorekeeper

Maintains the official game record and coordinates scoring information.

Penalty Box

Tracks penalties, penalty times, and player releases.

Game Statistics

Records player and team statistics throughout the game.

Other OIO Positions

Follow the specific responsibilities assigned by the OIO supervisor.

During the Game

  • Stay focused on your assigned responsibilities.
  • Communicate clearly with other officials.
  • Ask the supervisor if you are unsure about a situation.
  • Keep all official information accurate and current.
  • Remain professional throughout the game.

Intermissions

Use intermissions to review your records, confirm information, and prepare for the next period.

Post-Game

  • Verify that all required statistics and records are complete.
  • Submit or finalize required game documentation.
  • Report any issues or unusual situations to the supervisor.
  • Make sure your assigned work area is left organized.

Need Help?

If you have a question or encounter a problem during a game, contact the Off-Ice Officials supervisor.
